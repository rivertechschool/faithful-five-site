BOOKS AND LITERATURE
What we have written

Faithful Five and those who lead it produce their own written work — books, teaching materials, and translation. This page lists what is published and what is in preparation.

Published books.

2021

Man’s Place in God’s Plan — Crushed, Loved, and Crowned

Dan Hegelund

A book in three movements. The first brings comfort to a hurting heart, sharing the words that lifted the author’s spirit and restored his soul. The second is about the love of Christ — where the first part repairs a broken cup, the second fills it. The third is about a person’s place in this world once they have been mended and filled.

2012

The Voice

Linda Meissner

The autobiography of Linda Meissner, who worked among the gangs of New York alongside David Wilkerson in the years described in The Cross and the Switchblade, and who went on to found the Jesus People Army in the Pacific Northwest. The book tells how the Jesus People Movement began, how it travelled from New York to Seattle and then abroad, and how it came to an end. Dan Hegelund served as its editor. ISBN 978-91-980654-0-4.

In preparation.

Writing is ongoing rather than occasional. Work currently in preparation includes a fresh English translation of the Scriptures undertaken by Dan Hegelund, a second full-length work on integrative Christian life, and a collection of parables.
